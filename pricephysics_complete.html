Reconciliation rules

  • Match invoice and credit note by item, customer, date and reference.
  • Normalize units of measure and pack conversions.
  • Separate recoverable GST from commercial revenue/cost.
  • Use cost effective on transaction date, not today's cost.
  • Flag missing exchange rates, cost layers and allocation basis.

Interpretation rules

Keep revenue deductions and cost-to-serve components separate.

Important: GST collected on behalf of government is not revenue. Credit notes, returns and post-sale rebates reduce realized revenue when attributable to the sale. Freight charged to the customer may be revenue; freight borne by the seller is a selling cost. Configure treatment consistently with accounting policy.

What PricePhysics will not assume

Guard against misleading recommendations.

  • Lowest competitor price is not automatically the right price.
  • Historical correlation is not proof of causation.
  • Price difference is not leakage until cost and terms are comparable.
  • Elasticity is estimated only from sufficient, clean observations.
  • Competitor data must have a source, observation date and confidence score.
